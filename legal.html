Where the data comes from

Team, event and match data come from FTCScout, a community site that collects it from the FIRST Tech Challenge Events API. AutoScout downloads it when you load or refresh an event, and keeps a copy on your device so it works offline.

Data accuracy

CAP, OPR, EPA, XPR, the match predictions, win chances, projected standings, alliance tables and every other figure AutoScout shows are calculated by AutoScout from that data. They are estimates for scouting and general interest, not official results, and are not provided or checked by FIRST or FTCScout. Data can be late, incomplete or wrong, especially during a live event. Check official FIRST sources before relying on a number for a competition decision.

What is stored, and where

  • No ads, analytics or tracking. AutoScout uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • On your device. Loaded events, saved sessions, your scouting notes and photos, the FTCScout data cache and your settings (such as dark mode) are kept in your browser's own storage. They stay on that device unless you download a session file or share them with a team account.
  • Team accounts (optional). If you create or sign in to a team account, the account name (a team number or username) and a scrambled password check are stored, never the password itself. Anything you share with the account (notes, photos, chat messages, pick lists and sessions) is stored in a public GitHub repository that anyone can read. Don't share personal or private information there. Your sign-in is remembered on your device for up to 12 hours.
  • Admins. AutoScout's administrators can view, edit or remove shared data and accounts, for moderation, account recovery and removal requests.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and team accounts run through a Cloudflare service. Like almost any website, these may log standard request details (IP address, browser type, time) for security and troubleshooting. AutoScout does not use them to build profiles, and does not sell or share them.
  • Other services the site loads from. Opening AutoScout also contacts jsDelivr (to download the calculation engine), Google Fonts (for the font) and FTCScout (for event data). Each sees a normal web request from your device.

Third-party links

AutoScout links to FTCScout, FIRST's own sites and GitHub. They run independently and have their own privacy practices, which AutoScout doesn't control.

Reporting an issue

To report a problem, or to ask for shared data or an account to be corrected or removed, open an issue on AutoScout's GitHub page, or contact LightSaders Robotics through the AutoScout website. Removal requests should come from an official team contact.
